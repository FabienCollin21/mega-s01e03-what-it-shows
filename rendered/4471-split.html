The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it. It reaches every tenant and every channel.

hint: booking-v3’s date field asks for the wrong order. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the field, so people type month first, and the form reads the tenant’s order, day first. The result looks the same as parser, a swapped day and month, so the subject lines match and it was merged. It only comes from the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it: the hint and the form disagree with each other.

one-day move: dates land one day off. These reports are not a swap. A Tuesday arrives as a Monday, a meeting shows on different days for a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto, an invite goes out before the booking was made, one month is off by a day. That points at a time-zone conversion, not at the date order. The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json); causes.ts still files them under their tenant’s cause.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone, and all three problems produce the same subjects: “Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”. Intake’s tag does not split them either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the cause depends on the tenant and the form they run. The table counts each report once, by id, because the export repeats some rows.

For the desk

  • Do not close #4471’s reports with the parser fix alone. Check hint and one-day move against their own tickets.
  • When a report’s body does not show a day and month swap, it is not a #4471 duplicate, whatever its subject says.
